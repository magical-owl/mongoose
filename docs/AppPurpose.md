# App Purpose

## Core Positioning

Mongoose is a private memory space for real life.

The app helps people capture diary entries, photo moments, moods, reflections, reactions, stickers, and journals without turning those memories into public content.

## Why People Would Use It

People want to remember their life without the pressure of performing for an audience.

Some memories are meaningful but too personal, unfinished, vulnerable, or intimate for social media. Mongoose gives users a quieter place to keep those memories for themselves.

## Privacy Motivation

Many users take personal photos of themselves, loved ones, private spaces, relationships, daily routines, travel, outfits, and emotional moments.

When those photos are posted online, they can be copied, scraped, reposted, impersonated, used for fraud, or altered into deceptive or explicit content by bad actors. A private diary encourages a safer habit: capture the memory, keep it beautifully, and avoid exposing it publicly by default.

## Product Promise

Capture your days without sharing them with the world.

Mongoose is designed for memories that should be kept, not posted.

## User Value

- Keep intimate memories without posting them publicly.
- Save photo moments while on the go, even without writing much.
- Reflect later when there is more time and emotional space.
- Track moods, reactions, visits, and patterns privately.
- Organize memories by journals instead of social feeds.
- Preserve personal photos with context, not performance.
- Avoid the risks of oversharing on social platforms.
- Make journaling feel visual, warm, and expressive.

## Store Copy Draft

Capture your days without sharing them with the world. Keep private diary entries, photo moments, moods, reflections, and personal memories in one quiet space designed for you, not an audience.

## Privacy-Focused Copy Draft

Some memories are worth keeping, not posting. Save your photos, thoughts, and reflections privately, away from social feeds where personal images can be copied, misused, or taken out of context.

## Product Decision Filter

When evaluating new features, prefer choices that:

- Help users preserve private memories.
- Reduce pressure to post publicly.
- Make photo-first and writing-first journaling both feel natural.
- Keep sensitive personal content under user control.
- Avoid social-media-style comparison, performance, or public validation loops.
- Strengthen trust, privacy, and emotional safety.
